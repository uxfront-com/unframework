The preview has a new page. Its sidebar holds your examples as a tree of their categories, with a filter, and your components below them. The canvas shows the selection's title and description, with every target side by side or one at a time, and a docked panel holds the controls. The page follows your system's light or dark setting, keeps the theme you pick, and works from the keyboard: press `?` to list the shortcuts.
